# Code of Conduct

Locomi contributors are expected to make participation respectful and welcoming. Be considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, and accept constructive feedback in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Include relevant links or context. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and respond with a clear next step.
